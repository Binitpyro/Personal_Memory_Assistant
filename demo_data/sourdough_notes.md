# Sourdough notes

My starter is called Bramble. It lives in the fridge and gets fed once a week at
a 1:5:5 ratio (starter : flour : water by weight).

## The everyday loaf
- 500 g bread flour, 50 g whole wheat
- 72 % hydration, so 396 g water
- 100 g active starter, 11 g salt

Mix, rest 45 minutes, then four sets of stretch-and-folds half an hour apart.
Bulk ferment until the dough has grown by about half, usually five hours in a
warm kitchen. Shape, then cold-proof in the fridge overnight.

## Baking
Preheat the oven and the cast-iron pot to 250 °C. Bake 20 minutes with the lid
on at 230 °C, then 22 minutes with the lid off. Let it cool for at least an hour
before cutting, or the crumb turns gummy.

## What went wrong
At 80 % hydration the loaf spread flat. 72 % is the most I can shape cleanly.
