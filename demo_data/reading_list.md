# Reading list

- "The Salt Cartographer" - a novel about a mapmaker on a shrinking island.
  Slow start, wonderful last third. 4/5.
- "Small Engines, Large Rooms" - essays on repair culture. The chapter on
  sewing machines is the best one. 5/5.
- "Night Shift at the Observatory" - short stories. Uneven. 3/5.
