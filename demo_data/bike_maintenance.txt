Bike maintenance record - grey commuter bike

Chain: replaced at 3,400 km after the wear gauge showed 0.75 percent stretch.
The new chain is an 8-speed one. Check it again at 6,000 km.

Tyre pressure: 4.5 bar in the front and 5.0 bar in the rear. Lower in winter,
about 4.0 bar, for grip on wet leaves.

Brakes: the rear pads were replaced in October. The front pads still have about
2 mm left, so they need replacing before the end of the year.

Lights: the rear light runs on two AAA batteries, which last about six weeks of
daily commuting.

Cleaning routine: rinse the drivetrain after every wet ride, dry it with a rag,
and add one drop of chain oil per link. Wipe off the excess the next morning.
