# Weekend trip to Halden Cove

## Getting there
The train leaves at 07:42 on Saturday and takes about two and a half hours,
with one change at the junction. Return train on Sunday at 16:10.

## Where to stay
A small hostel above the harbour. Check-in from 15:00, so leave the bags at the
station lockers in the morning. Breakfast is included; towels cost extra.

## Plan
- Saturday: the cliff path to the lighthouse, about 9 km there and back. Take
  water, there is nowhere to buy any on the path.
- Saturday evening: the fish market closes at 18:00.
- Sunday morning: the tide pools are best at low tide, around 10:30 that day.

## Packing
Rain jacket, walking shoes, phone charger, a printed copy of the hostel booking.
