# Apartment lease - summary

The lease runs for twelve months and renews automatically unless either side
gives notice.

- Rent: 940 per month, due on the 3rd of each month.
- Late fee: 25 if the rent arrives after the 8th.
- Deposit: two months' rent, returned within 30 days of moving out, minus any
  repairs beyond normal wear.
- Notice period: 60 days in writing before moving out. Email to the building
  office counts as writing.
- Pets: one cat or one small dog with written permission.
- Repairs: report through the tenant portal. Emergencies such as a leak or no
  heating go to the 24-hour maintenance line instead.

The radiator in the bedroom was already dented when I moved in. I took photos on
move-in day so it is not taken out of the deposit.
