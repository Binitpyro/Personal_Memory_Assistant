Laptop battery notes

The battery report says the battery holds 82 percent of its original capacity
after two years. On a full charge it lasts about four hours of note-taking with
the screen at half brightness, and under two hours when compiling code.

To slow the wear down:
- Charge limit set to 80 percent in the manufacturer's settings app.
- Unplug it once a week and let it run down to about 30 percent.
- Keep it off the bed and the sofa; it gets hot when the vents are covered.

The power-saving mode cuts the screen refresh rate, which is the biggest single
gain. Turning off the keyboard backlight helps a little.

If capacity drops below 70 percent, a replacement battery is cheaper than a new
laptop. Check the exact model number on the sticker underneath before ordering.
