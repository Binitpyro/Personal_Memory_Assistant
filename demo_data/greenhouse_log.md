# Greenhouse log

## Ventilation
The roof vents are on wax-cylinder openers. They start to open at 27 °C and are
fully open by 32 °C. On still days above 30 °C I also prop the door open, because
the vents alone cannot pull enough air through.

## Tomatoes
This season I grew two varieties: "Copperline", a small orange cherry type, and
"Ridgeback", a ribbed beefsteak. Copperline cropped from mid-July and out-yielded
Ridgeback by roughly three to one. Ridgeback split badly after the heavy rain in
August, so next year I will water it less and more often.

## Watering
Morning watering only, so the leaves dry before evening. The drip line runs for
12 minutes at 06:30. In September I cut it to 8 minutes.

## Next year
- Start seeds on the windowsill in the first week of March.
- Put the shade cloth up in June, not July.
- Try basil between the tomato rows to save bench space.
